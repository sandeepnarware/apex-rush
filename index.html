Controls

How to Play

Keyboard

  • ↑W Accelerate
  • ↓S Brake
  • ←→AD Steer
  • SpaceShift Nitro
  • EscP Pause

Gamepad

  • RTA Accelerate
  • LTB Brake
  • L-StickD-Pad Steer
  • XRB Nitro
  • Start Pause / resume
  • D-PadAB Navigate menus
  • LBRB Switch car / circuit

Touch

  • Tilt steering: hold the phone like a wheel and rotate it — centre is set automatically at the start lights
  • Or turn tilt off in Settings and use the ◀ ▶ arrows
  • Right thumb: gas, brake & N₂O
  • Auto-accelerate is on by default for touch

Tips

  • Yellow chevrons warn of sharp bends — lift or brake before them.
  • Grass and sand slow you down; trees and rocks stop you dead.
  • Nitro refills slowly. Save it for straights and overtakes.
